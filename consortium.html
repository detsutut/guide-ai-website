Consortium

GUIDE-AI brings together a multidisciplinary European consortium of academic institutions, clinical centres, technology companies, and patient organisations.

Who We Are

Partner Institutions

Our consortium unites 19 partners from 8 countries — Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Estonia, Slovenia, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Israel — spanning academic research, clinical practice, technology, social sciences, and patient representation.

Project Structure

Governance

GUIDE-AI is governed by a lean structure designed to enable fast decision-making while maintaining scientific integrity and IHI JU compliance.

Decision-Making Bodies

General Assembly This body meets annually and constitutes the formal decision-making authority on strategic matters.
Steering Committee This committee meets monthly and is responsible for day-to-day project governance, milestone tracking, and cross-WP coordination.
Project Coordinator Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin (Germany).
Scientific Advisory Board Independent external experts providing strategic input and assessing alignment with clinical needs and regulatory requirements.
